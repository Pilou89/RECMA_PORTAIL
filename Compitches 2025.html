This is the 26th edition of Compitches, the
“competitiveness in pitches” report. Compitches is a
performance index, providing insights regarding the
media agency networks’ dynamism and performance.
The research is based on 1,827 participations to
pitches in 45 countries, involving 697 agencies. The
goal of this report is not to analyze all the moves
recorded by RECMA over the year, but rather those
above a certain threshold in each country, as well as
those where a pitch or competitive review took place.
Each network is assessed at country level (from A+ to
C), revealing which are the most robust ones: a useful
homogeneity index for advertisers as well as for HQs
revealing a different outcome from the New Biz
Balance (NBB), a report published beginning of May
2026, based on adspends only.
Compitches & NBB, two distinct yet complementary
metrics for evaluating media agencies recent
performance
Unlike the New Business Balance, which focuses solely
on the spend of accounts won or lost, the Compitches
take a broader approach. Every account move is
analyzed and evaluated based on the level of
competitive challenge, considering factors such as
incumbents, competing agencies, the nature of the
assignment, spends, and more.
Moreover, Compitches rankings are aggregating three
years, delivering a hierarchy which reflects the average
pitch cycle in the industry.

Perimeter
How many agencies are evaluated in this report?
The perimeter of the report includes 697 agencies evaluated in 45 markets with 64
major independents and 21 standalone agencies part of the international media
Groups.
How many pitches are analyzed?
Pitches are registered regarding the year 2025 based on the date of announcement
and not the date of effectiveness. Retentions are taken into account in the study.
Conversely, moves without a pitch or formal competition with identified
competitors and adspends that fall below the specific threshold defined for each
market, as well as intra-group transfers, do not receive any point in the Compitches
study. For global or regional pitches, only local agencies that are highly involved in
the competition are rewarded with points. This usually reflects the significance of
the market for the advertiser conducting the global or regional review.
Based on the criteria related to the perimeter outlined above, we registered
1,827 pitches by aggregating all moves that met each market’s criteria.
Overall, the scope of this Compitches report focuses on 43% of all the moves
identified in 2025.
How are group-level wins accounted for?
For the purpose of this report, Group-level wins via dedicated units are most often
attributed to a lead agency in agreement with the groups in the relevant countries.
