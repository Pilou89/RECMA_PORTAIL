01

2025 was a moderately active year in Hong Kong, with notable local reviews: Nestle to Spark Foundry, AS Watson to Hearts & Science, OSIM to UM, alongside a high volume of retentions including Vitasoy, HK Disneyland, KAO and ASAHI. Regional and global mandates play a secondary role: most strategic decisions are made elsewhere, with Hong Kong largely receiving the outcome.

02

The volume of retentions partly explains the stability at the top, where Starcom leads for a second consecutive year. The biggest movement is downward: Carat falls four places to 6th, weighed down by the loss of AS Watson, while Havas slips two ranks. Conversely, PHD and OMD both gain ground.

03

The OMG-IPG merger makes Omnicom Media the new benchmark in Hong Kong. Combining OMD, PHD and the newly ranked Hearts & Science, the group leads in pitch volume and wins. Alongside Publicis Media, both groups stand clearly ahead of the rest, consistent with the global picture that emerged from the merger.

04

WPP Media faces the most difficult year, posting a negative new business balance driven by a high number of departures. Mindshare and Wavemaker have yet to find their footing after several years of transition, a pattern observed across several Asian markets.

05

Group client portfolios reveal distinct profiles:

  • Publicis leads the new business balance alongside Omnicom Media, but its portfolio relies heavily on international accounts won through global pitches, leaving the group with a more limited local presence than its score would suggest.
  • Omnicom Media now offers the most balanced profile.
  • Havas stands out for its local depth and diversified book.
  • WPP Media continues to show a structural imbalance between wins and departures, increasingly reflected in its quali-score trajectory.
