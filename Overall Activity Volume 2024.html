01Key findings

Executive summary from the global report.

01

Industry growth

The media agency industry grew by 6.2% in 2024, compared with +5.8% in 2023, across RECMA’s perimeter of six international media groups, standalones and major independents.

OMD maintains the top position ahead of EssenceMediacom and Mindshare, with a significantly higher growth rate than direct competitors.

03

WPP Media still leads by group

New business impact remains selective

2025 forecast

Acquisition of IPG by OMG
The acquisition of IPG by OMG, announced in Dec. 2024 and
expected to be finalized in the second half of 2025, marks a historic
event in the communications sector. While OMG had already
considered a merger with Publicis Groupe in 2013, this deal exceeds
by far recent major acquisitions, such as those of Sapient or Epsilon
by Publicis, or Aegis and Merkle by dentsu.
This event is historic for several reasons: on one hand, due to its
financial scale and on the other, because it fundamentally reshapes
the structure of the industry. This "New OMG" is not yet finalized but
is forecasted to be the industry leader, by far. However, as this report
focuses on 2024, OMG and IPG are evaluated separately but we have
added a line in our global and regional group rankings for reference,
to reflect what this new group would hypothetically look like.
•
GroupM is now WPP Media
Launched in a major restructuring operation in 2024 aimed at better
integrating the various agencies and capabilities of the British group,
GroupM has been rebranded as WPP Media since mid-2025. This
trend follows the "Power of One" model introduced by Publicis
several years ago or “one dentsu” last year. While this rebranding has
only been effective for a few weeks, RECMA uses the WPP Media
nomenclature throughout its report to refer to the activities of the
former GroupM in 2024.

The weight of new business on agency evaluations
A strong boost for Spark Foundry and OMG agencies, but a significant setback
for EssenceMediacom
In order to identify the agencies that won or lost the most billings through client
wins or losses in this 2024 report, RECMA has calculated a pro-rated balance of
accounts lost between Q3 2023 and Q2 2024, the period with the most
significant impact. As a matter of fact, accounts won or lost prior to Q2 2023
were already, largely, reflected in last year’s evaluation, while those after Q2
2024 will have a bigger impact into next year’s analysis due to the date of
effectiveness (see next page: Forecast).

9- How RECMA evaluates the dedicated units at the group level?
RECMA evaluates dedicated units at the group level using several methodological
principles, aiming to best reflect the actual organization and account management.
1. Affiliation to a Lead Network Agency
When a dedicated is clearly affiliated with a main network then its volume billings is attributed to
that agency. Example: Plateform GSK, the dedicated unit created by Publicis to manage
GlaxoSmithKline, is affiliated to Zenith.
2. Specific Treatment for Multi-Network Management
If the dedicated unit is managed by several networks depending on the market, then RECMA assigns
the spends of the dedicated unit to the relevant agency based on local information. Example:
OpenMind, the dedicated unit created by WPP Media for Nestlé, is mostly affiliated with Mindshare,
but in some markets, like Germany, it's affiliated with EssenceMediacom.
3. Rare case where the dedicated unit remains at the group level.
This happens in some cases, particularly in the USA, where several large accounts may be managed
at the group level without a global network acting as the lead media agency. For example, this is the
